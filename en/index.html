Why TxConv?

TxConv is designed for small tasks that should not require an account, an installation, or uploading sensitive text. Every tool states what it does and keeps its controls separate from any advertising. Open a tool, paste your text, copy the result, and close the tab.

How the tools work

Each tool is a single page that runs in your browser using plain JavaScript. When you press a button, the conversion happens on your device and the result appears on the same page. The text you enter in a tool is not sent to a TxConv server, and nothing you type is saved after you close the page.

What each tool is good for

  • JSON Formatter & Validator – check an API response, tidy a configuration file, or minify JSON for a one-line field. The page also explains which JavaScript quirks, such as large-number precision, can affect the result.
  • Case Converter – rename a field or variable between camelCase, snake_case, PascalCase, and kebab-case when you move between languages, databases, and URLs.
  • Base64 Encoder & Decoder – encode or decode UTF-8 text, and read URL-safe Base64 such as the parts of a JSON Web Token. The page explains why Base64 is not a way to protect secrets.

Advertising and analytics

TxConv is free. To cover running costs, the site may show ads from Google AdSense and uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. These services do not receive the text you enter in the tools. Details, including how to opt out of personalized ads, are in the privacy policy.

Need a Japanese tool?

The Japanese edition offers additional utilities for Japanese text, tax calculation, Markdown, CSV, and Salesforce API names. These include full-width/half-width conversion, hiragana/katakana conversion, character counting, text diff, password generation, and more.
